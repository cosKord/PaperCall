## 5. How the game works

- **Calls.** A call is your Hit or Miss pick on a market, with a number of points.
- **Closing.** Calls close [30] minutes before the exam begins. The server's clock is the only clock that counts. A call that reaches us at or after the closing time is rejected.
- **Final.** Once you place a call, you can't change or cancel it.
- **Hit.** A market is a Hit if at least one question in the paper requires that topic to be solved, even in part. Everything else is a Miss.
- **Settlement.** After the exam, an admin uploads the question paper and marks each market Hit, Miss or Void. Points are paid out from the pool for that market, or refunded if a market is voided or nobody backed the winning side.
- **Mistakes.** If we find an error in a result or a payout, we may correct it and adjust points to match. We will explain what we changed.
- **Disputes.** If you think a result is wrong, write to [contact email] within [7] days. An admin will review it, and our decision after review is final for game purposes.
