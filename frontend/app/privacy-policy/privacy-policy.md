# Privacy Policy

**PaperCall** · Effective date: 3/11/2026 · Last updated: 3/11/2026

PaperCall is a free, student-run game where students use virtual points to predict what will appear in their exam papers. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, who can see it, and what control you have. We aim to follow India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and its Rules.

**Who is responsible for your data:** PaperCall, a student project at KJSSE. We are not run or endorsed by KJSSE in any way whatsoever.

**Contact and grievances:** Grievance contact: Dnyanit, dnyanitbhusari21@gmail.com.

---

## 1. What we collect

**Account details you give us**
- Personal email address used to sign in, or the email address associated with your Google account if you choose Google sign-in
- Name and username
- Branch and year
- Passwords are handled by Supabase Auth and are not stored in PaperCall's application database

**What you do on PaperCall**
- Your calls: which market, Hit or Miss, how many points, and when
- Your points history: grants, stakes, payouts and refunds
- Results and stats built from the above (balance, profit and loss, hit rate)

**Technical data**
- IP address, browser and device type, and request times, kept in server logs for security, abuse prevention and fixing bugs
- A session cookie that keeps you logged in

**What we do not collect:** phone numbers, payment details, government IDs, precise location, contacts, or photos. We do not run advertising or third-party tracking.

## 2. Why we use it

- To create your account and let you sign in with your personal email or Google account
- To run the game: accept calls, close markets on time, settle results, and calculate points and leaderboards
- To keep the game fair: spot duplicate accounts, bots, and abuse
- To help you if something goes wrong, and to fix problems
- To send essential account messages, such as email confirmation and important notices

We use your data only for these purposes. We do not sell it.

**Your consent.** By creating an account you agree to this policy. You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account (see section 7). Some features, like making calls, cannot work without the data above.

## 3. Who can see what

| Who | What they can see |
|---|---|
| Other players | Your username and your standing on the leaderboard. |
| Other players | They cannot see your name, email, or your individual calls. |
| Game admins | Account details (name, email, branch, year), your calls and your points history, because they run and settle the game |
| Only you | Your own profile, calls and stats |

## 4. Who we share data with

We share data only with the services that help us run PaperCall:
- **Database and authentication:** Supabase
- **Hosting:** Vercel
- **Google sign-in:** Google

These providers process data on our behalf and only to provide their service. They may store it on servers outside India, in EU. We may also disclose data if the law requires it, or if needed to protect the game and its players from serious abuse.

We never sell your data or share it for advertising.

## 5. How long we keep it

- Your account data: for as long as your account is active.
- If you delete your account, we delete or anonymise your personal data within [30] days. We may keep past calls and results in anonymised form so that other players' results and the leaderboard history stay correct.
- Backups are overwritten on a rolling basis, within about [30] days.
- Server logs: up to [90] days.

## 6. Security

We protect your data with encrypted connections (HTTPS), hashed passwords, access controls, and restricted admin access. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your data, we will tell you and explain what happened and what to do.

## 7. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to:
- Show you the personal data we hold about you
- Correct it, if it is wrong or out of date (for example, your branch)
- Delete it, by deleting your account
- Withdraw your consent
- Handle a complaint about how we treated your data

## 8. Cookies

We use one essential cookie to keep you logged in. It is not used for tracking or advertising. If we add analytics later, we will update this policy first.

## 9. Age

PaperCall is for students aged 18 or over. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 18, we will remove it and delete the data.

## 10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we will update the date at the top and show a notice on the site. Continuing to use PaperCall after a change means you accept the update.
